# 1. Business understanding

**Purpose:** understand the objectives and requirements from a business perspective, turn them into a data science
problem and plan how to reach them.

[CRISP-DM overview](README.md) · [Next: 2. Data understanding →](2-data-understanding.md)

## 1.1 Determine business objectives

### Background

_What is the business situation today, and what problem or opportunity started this project?_

### Business objectives

_What does the business want to achieve? State the primary objective and the related business questions._

### Business success criteria

_How will the business judge the project a success? Use measurable criteria and name who decides._
